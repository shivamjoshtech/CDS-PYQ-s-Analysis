## 💡 EXPERT TIPS FOR PREPARATION

### For Verbal Ability:
- Practice RC passages of 200–300 words on Science, History, Nature topics
- Learn at least 10 idioms per week from standard lists
- Focus on grammar: Subject-Verb Agreement, Articles, Prepositions
- Practice sentence rearrangement daily — look for logical + grammatical flow

### For Numerical Ability:
- Master Profit/Loss formulas including "profit on SP" type questions
- Clock problems appear 2 times every paper — learn angle formula: |30H – 11M/2|
- Practice mixture/allegation and CI/SI together
- Speed mental math for percentage calculations

### For General Awareness:
- Follow monthly current affairs (especially Indian defence news)
- Know all DRDO missiles: types, range, launch platform
- Study Indian military exercises (bilateral + multilateral)
- Revise Indian history: Mughal period, freedom movement, post-independence
- Know all classical dances, their states

### For Reasoning:
- Practice Venn diagram daily (number-based + class-based)
- For coding: try reverse+shift, shift+skip patterns
- Figure series: look for rotation, reflection, addition of elements
- Blood relations: draw family tree for complex questions
