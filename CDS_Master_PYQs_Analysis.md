## Part 0 — Introduction & How This Document Was Built

Yeh document paanch CDS question-paper analyses (CDS-II 2023, CDS-II 2024, CDS-I 2025, CDS-II 2025, aur CDS-I 2026) ko ek single master file mein merge karta hai. Har ek topic, har ek sub-topic, aur har ek section ko teeno papers — English, General Knowledge, aur Elementary Mathematics — ke andar cover kiya gaya hai. Koi bhi topic kisi bhi uploaded file se chhoda nahi gaya hai.

Sabse important baat: har topic ko cross-match kiya gaya hai — yani dekha gaya hai ki woh topic kitne saalon mein (5 mein se kitne papers mein) repeat hua hai. Jo topic saare 5 papers mein har baar aata hai, woh sabse zyada important hai (guaranteed/recurring). Jo topic sirf 1-2 papers mein dikha, woh occasional/rotating maana gaya hai.

### 0.3 Cross-Matching Method Used

Har subject/topic ke liye yeh check kiya gaya:

1. Kya yeh topic saare 5 papers mein hai? → ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MUST-STUDY** (guaranteed every year)
2. Kya yeh topic 3-4 papers mein hai? → ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **HIGH PRIORITY** (very likely to repeat)

### 1.3 GRAMMAR (Error Spotting + Parts of Speech + Prepositions) — 5/5 Papers

Grammar combined (Error Spotting + Word Class + Prepositions) English paper ka doosra sabse bada chunk hai — 25-45 questions tak (har paper mein alag weightage).

| Paper | Error Spotting | Parts of Speech | Prepositions/Det. | Grammar Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 30 Qs (25%) |
| 2024 | 15 | 10 | 10 | 45 Qs (37.5%) — highest ever |
| 2025-I | 10 | 10 | 5 | 25 Qs (~21%) |
| 2025-II | 10 | 15 | 5 | 30 Qs (25%) |
| 2026 | 5 | 10 | 10 | 25 Qs (~21%) |

**Error Spotting — Key Grammar Areas Tested (across all years)**
- Subject-Verb Agreement — most recurring error type (every single paper)
- Pronoun reference/agreement errors
- Tense errors, especially reported speech tense-backshift
- Wrong preposition usage
- Comparative structure errors
- Passive voice misuse
- Infinitive/verb-form errors
- Important trap: "No Error" is frequently the correct answer (2025-II noted 7 out of 10 in one section) — don't over-hunt for mistakes that aren't there.

**Parts of Speech / Word Forms — Pattern**
Yeh section root-word families test karta hai — ek hi root se Noun/Verb/Adjective/Adverb forms banana. Yeh format 5/5 papers mein guaranteed 10 questions ka block hai.
Example word families seen across papers: Intend→Intention/Intend/Intentional; Experiment→Experimentation/Experiment/Experimental; Discriminate→Discrimination/Discriminate/Discriminatory; Justice→Justice/Justify/Just/Juridical; qualify→qualification/qualifying; err→error/erroneous/erroneously.

**Prepositions / Determiners — Pattern**
Distributive determiners (each/every/all/any), quantifiers (further/several/most), prepositions of time/place/purpose, phrasal verbs (fall back on), correlative conjunctions (no sooner...than). Concessive/causal prepositions (in spite of/despite/notwithstanding/on account of) were especially dominant in CDS-II 2023 (5 out of 10 in that section).

Interesting pattern: CDS papers rotate between Cloze Test aur Reading Comprehension format — kabhi dono, kabhi sirf ek.

|---|---|---|

*Prep strategy: Practice BOTH formats — cloze fill-ups need grammar+vocabulary-in-context skill, while RC needs analytical/inferential reading. ### 1.7 STATEMENT/SENTENCE RELATIONSHIP (S1-S2 Logic) — 3/5 Papers (2025 onwards trend)

Yeh format judge karta hai ki Statement 2, Statement 1 ko confirm/contradict/extend/reinforce karta hai ya nahi. 2025-I, 2025-II, aur 2026 mein 10 questions ka fixed block hai — yeh ek newer addition hai jo 2025 se shuru hua.

Content vehicles seen: Albatross wingspan, scientific theory/hypothesis, India's liberalisation economy, 10,000-hours mastery rule, India's democracy, rose as poetic symbol (2025-I); failure/success, Big Bang, climate science, bottled water, sports popularity, forest cover, Indian diaspora (2025-II).

### 1.8 OTHER RECURRING FORMATS

**Match the Lists (Vocabulary-based) — 4/5 papers**
List I-List II matching format using vocabulary/word-meaning pairs with coded answers. Present in 2024 (Q101-105, GK-vocab hybrid), 2025-I (Q71-80), 2025-II (Q101-110), 2026 (Q101-110).

**Confusable / Homophone Words — explicit in 3/5, folded into vocabulary elsewhere**
Word triplets/pairs that sound alike but differ in meaning (e.g., Dairy/Dreary/Diary; Pare/Pair/Pear). Explicit dedicated sections in 2025-I, 2025-II, 2026.

**Discourse Markers/Connectors — 2/5 papers (2025 onwards)**
Therefore/thus, somewhat/indeed/fortunately, in particular/as a matter of fact, significantly/unexpectedly — testing logical connector choice in academic/argumentative register. Seen only in 2025-I and 2025-II so far — a possible newer recurring fixture to watch for.

**Paper-specific One-off Formats**
- Voice Change (Active↔Passive) — only in 2024 (Q116-120, 5 Qs)
- Combining Two Sentences via Connectors — only in 2024 (Q106-110, 5 Qs)

### 1.10 English — Key Strategic Takeaways (All 5 Papers Combined)

1. Vocabulary difficulty is rising year-on-year — 2023/24 tested editorial-level words, while 2025/26 papers test GRE/CAT/UPSC-level words (malversation, palimpsest, imbroglio, extirpate). Build a daily advanced-word list and revise continuously.
2. Grammar (Error Spotting + Parts of Speech + Prepositions) consistently totals 25-45 questions — this is rule-based and highly learnable; master Subject-Verb Agreement, tense, and pronoun-reference rules first.
3. Jumbles (Sentence + Para) are GS-flavored every single year — History, Geography, Economy, Polity, and Science topics are the raw material. Strong GS reading directly speeds up jumble-solving.
4. Cloze and Reading Comprehension alternate — practice both formats since you cannot predict which one will appear.
5. "No Error" is a frequent correct answer in Error Spotting — resist the urge to find a mistake where none exists.
6. Newer formats (Statement Relationship, Discourse Markers) emerged from 2025 onwards — these may be becoming permanent fixtures, so don't ignore them as "one-off."
7. Idioms remain a fixed, learnable, high-ROI 10-question block every year — a list of 200+ idioms (classic + modern) covers this comfortably.

8. **Vocabulary difficulty is escalating every year:** build an advanced/GRE-level word list and revise daily, since 2025-26 papers are noticeably harder than 2023-24 in this area.
