## 📌 Detailed Analysis — English Sections

### 1️⃣ VOCABULARY / WORD SUBSTITUTION (Q1–10) — 10 Questions
**क्या पूछा जाता है:** Underlined word को सबसे उचित alternative से replace करो

**इस paper में tested words:**
Intimidated, Perspicacity, Sumptuous, Lure, Induction, Opprobrium, Enormousness, Laudatory, Scurrilous, Tumult

**Key Focus:** Formal/advanced English synonyms, near-synonyms, context-based word choice

---

### 2️⃣ ERROR DETECTION (Q11–20) — 10 Questions
**क्या पूछा जाता है:** Sentence के किस part में grammatical error है

**इस paper में tested errors:**
- Subject-verb agreement ("mistakes…was" — plural needs "were")
- Wrong word choice ("imperial" should be "imperious")
- Hanged vs Hung (clothes are hung, people are hanged)
- Pronoun agreement errors
- Incorrect preposition usage

**Grammar Areas:** Subject-verb agreement, pronoun reference, correct word form, tense

## 🔑 Key Observations — English Paper

1. **Vocabulary = 25% of Paper** — Word substitution + matching + usage + pairs + homophones = ~30 questions. Single most important area.
2. **Grammar tested indirectly** — No dedicated grammar section, but Error Detection + Word Order + Word Forms + Cloze = 40 questions (1/3rd of paper).
3. **NCERT content in Jumbles** — Para & sentence jumbles directly sourced from NCERT Geography, History, Economics. NCERT readers have natural advantage.
4. **Advanced vocabulary** — Cuneiform, Chutzpah, Ochlocracy, Hoplarchy, Sangfroid — regular The Hindu editorial reading is essential.
5. **Latin/Legal terms featured** — Suo moto, Ex gratia, Locus standi, Fait accompli — must know common legal Latin.
6. **No Reading Comprehension passage** — Cloze test partially compensates. Analytical skills tested via Statement Relationship instead.

## 🎯 Aapke Liye Personal Tips (Meri Taraf Se)

### ✅ Jo karna CHAHIYE:
- **NCERT pehle, baaki baad mein** — GK + English dono ke liye NCERT gold mine hai
- **Negative marking ka darr nahi, par andhaa guess mat karo** — 2 options eliminate ho jayein toh attempt karo

- **Current affairs sirf facts yaad karo** — concept nahi, direct facts

### ❌ Jo NAHI karna chahiye:
- Vocabulary sirf list se mat padho — context mein padho (The Hindu editorial)
- History dates ratta mat maro — events ka connection samjho
- Maths mein sab topics equally mat padho — Trig + Geometry mein zyada time do
- Mock tests skip mat karo — real exam speed ke liye zaroori hain

### ⏱️ Time Management in Exam:
- **Maths:** Pehle easy questions (Stats + Arithmetic) → fir Algebra → last mein tough Trig
- **GK:** Pehle known sections → skip unknown → end mein revisit
- **English:** Vocabulary + Idioms → Grammar → Jumbles → Cloze (passage last mein)
