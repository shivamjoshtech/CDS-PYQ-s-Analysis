### 🔑 Key Observations — English
- **Vocabulary is the single biggest area (~33%)** — synonyms, antonyms, word-pairs, and direct meanings combined dominate.
- Difficulty level: words like *iconoclast, equivocate, parsimonious, pachyderm, obsequious* — **advanced/GRE-level vocabulary** is being tested.
- Grammar section bhi substantial hai (~21%) — word class identification (gerund, participle, preposition vs adverb confusion) tricky hota hai.
- Confusable words section (crops/corps/corpse, censer/censor/censure, idyll/idle/idol) — ye purely **spelling-and-context** based hai, rote practice se improve hoga.
- Para-jumbles topics covered: Science history, Industrial Revolution, Feudalism, Japanese economy, Force & momentum, Biodiversity, Agriculture's origins — **general knowledge ke passages** use ho rahe hain jumbles ke liye.
- Reading comprehension was on **"Biophilia hypothesis" and ecopsychology** — a single conceptual passage with inference-based questions.

### 🔑 Key Observations — Mathematics
- **Geometry (20%) and Arithmetic (18%) dominate** — strongest focus areas for practice.
- **Comprehension/paired questions (Q81-100)** are a unique CDS pattern — master ek concept se 2 questions solve hote hain, time-efficient strategy banao.
- **Data Sufficiency (Q71-80)** is a fixed 10-question block testing logical reasoning with geometry/algebra — practice this format specifically, it's unlike typical MCQs.
- Trigonometry is heavily weighted (13%) with multiple identity-based comprehension sets.
- No direct coordinate geometry seen in this set, but heavy emphasis on triangle/circle theorems.

# 🎯 OVERALL CROSS-PAPER INSIGHTS (Mera Analysis)

## 1. GK paper sabse zyada "current and dynamic" hai
GK mein **Current Affairs (~17.5%) aur Geography (~18.3%)** combined almost 36% paper bana dete hain. Agar aap GK mein strong marks chahte ho, toh:
- **Daily current affairs** (especially government schemes, defence MoUs, international agreements, sports records) — last 6 months ka revision zaroori hai.
- **Physical + Indian Geography** ko NCERT Class 11-12 level pe thoroughly cover karo — rocks, weathering, soils, rivers, forests bohot specific detail mein puche jaate hain.

## 2. History mein Ancient India ka weightage badh gaya hai
Is paper mein **Ancient History (sites, inscriptions, manuscripts) ka focus** Modern History se zyada tha — NCERT "Themes in Indian History" (Class 12, Part 1) revise karo specifically.

## 3. Mathematics mein Geometry + Comprehension format practice karo
Geometry (20%) sabse bada chunk hai. Comprehension-based pairs (last 20 Qs) ek efficient strategy demand karte hain — ek concept clear hone par 2 marks guaranteed.

## 4. English mein Vocabulary-heavy prep zaroori
33% paper sirf vocabulary (synonym/antonym/word-meaning/word-pairs) pe based hai. **High-level vocabulary list** (GRE/CAT level words jaise erudite, obsequious, parsimonious) banao aur daily revise karo.

## 5. Defence Awareness — chhota but high-value section
Sirf 4 questions GK mein defence-specific hain (CDS Staff, training academies, Ministry of Defence subjects, DRDO), lekin yeh **CDS-specific unique content** hai jo coaching books mein kam milta hai — Lucent GK ya Defence Yearbook se cover karo.

## 6. Negative marking strategy
Har paper mein **1/3rd penalty** for wrong answer hai — Maths mein especially (1 mark per Q, no penalty for blank) **guessing avoid karo** jab tak elimination se 2 options tak na pahunch jao.
