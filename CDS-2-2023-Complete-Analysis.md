## C.2 English — Section-by-Section Topic Analysis

### Section 1: Parts of Speech (Q1–10)
| Sub-topic | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Verb identification | 3 |
| Adverb identification | 3 |
| Pronoun identification | 1 |
| Preposition identification | 2 |
| Interjection | 1 |

**Insight:** Verb/Adverb confusion sabse common trap hai; dual-class words (preposition vs adverb) test hote hain.

### Section 2: Fill in the Blanks (Q11–20)
**What's tested:** Vocabulary + contextual word choice (formal/academic register — legal, social, literary themes).

### Section 3: Ordering of Words — Jumbled Parts (Q21–30)
**Theme spread:** Theatre/Drama history, Political/Economic theory, Constitution & Law, Education.
**Insight:** 6/10 questions draw from Political Science/Constitutional Law/Sociology-style academic prose; Theatre history recurs 3 times.

### Section 4: Idioms and Phrases (Q31–40)
| Idiom | Category |
|---|---|
| Kettle of fish | Difficulty/contrast |
| Sitting on the fence | Indecision |
| Strained every nerve | Effort |
| At sea | Confusion |
| Gift of the gab | Speaking ability |
| Changed hands | Ownership transfer |
| Irons in the fire | Multiple tasks |
| Brain drain | Migration |
| Right as rain | Good health |
| All Greek to me | Incomprehension |

**Insight:** High-frequency, "must-know" idiom tier — no obscure idioms.

### Section 5: Spotting Errors (Q41–50)
**Focus areas:** Passive voice misuse, infinitive/verb-form errors, pronoun-antecedent errors, comparative structure errors. 2 questions deliberately have "No Error" — classic overconfidence trap.

### Section 6: Synonyms (Q51–60)
Words: Utopian, Nugatory, Convulsed, Complacent, Derisory, Defused, Floundered, Brevity, Obtuse, Transpired.
**Insight:** Editorial/opinion-piece English vocabulary level (The Hindu/Indian Express tier).

### Section 7: Ordering of Sentences — Para Jumbles (Q61–70)
**Theme spread:** Biology/Science, Technology, Sociology, Economics, Tribal Culture, Geography, History.
**Insight:** Massive interdisciplinary spread — testing GS reading comprehension disguised as English.

### Section 8: Antonyms (Q71–80)
Words: Invincible, Condensation, Ushered in, Obfuscated, Abomination, Infraction, Denounce, Fostered, Self-aggrandisement, Assertion.
**Insight:** Same academic non-fiction pool (History, Law, Sociology) as Sections 3 & 7.

### Section 9: Cloze Composition (Q81–90)
**Passage Theme:** Sociology of Social Movements (objectives, ideology, leadership, strategies).
**Skills tested:** Noun/verb/adjective choice in context, phrasal verbs, relative pronouns.

### Section 10: Prepositions (Q91–100)
**Dominant pattern:** Concessive & causal prepositions — "in spite of/despite/notwithstanding/on account of/as a consequence of" (5 out of 10 questions).

### Section 11: Completion of Sentence (Q101–110)
**Key grammar rules:** Subject-Verb Agreement, Correlative structures ("No sooner...than"), Reported speech tense-backshift, Uncountable noun agreement.

### Section 12: Comprehension (Q111–120)
**Passage I:** "Of Truth" — Francis Bacon (classic literary essay, archaic English) — theme, reasoning, literary devices (metaphor, personification), inference.
**Passage II:** Law, Legal Literacy & Custom (modern academic/legal essay) — author's view, central theme, vocabulary-in-context, synonym-in-context.
**Insight:** CDS consistently mixes one classic literary extract + one contemporary academic extract.

## 5. CROSS-PAPER STRATEGIC INSIGHTS

1. **Recurring format patterns across all 3 papers:**
   - GK & English dono mein "List I-List II matching" / "Statement-based assertion" formats common hain.
   - Maths mein Data Sufficiency last 10 Qs mein fixed; English mein Comprehension last 10 Qs mein fixed.
2. **Academic-prose overlap:** English ke jumbled/cloze/antonym passages Political Science, Sociology, History, Geography se aate hain — GK ki History/Geography/Polity prep English score ko bhi boost karegi.
3. **Current Affairs window:** GK ka Current Affairs section last 6-12 months ke events cover karta hai — exam se pehle ka 1 saal crucial.
4. **Negative marking sabhi papers mein same (1/3rd)** — Maths mein agar calculation pakka nahi, English mein agar word ka exact meaning pata nahi, GK mein agar specific fact pata nahi — to guess avoid karein jab tak elimination se 2 options tak na pahuch jaayein.
5. **Time management:** Teeno papers 2-2 hours ke hain. English mein vocabulary-based sections (Synonyms/Antonyms/Idioms) fastest solve hote hain — pehle attempt karke confidence aur time bachaya ja sakta hai.
